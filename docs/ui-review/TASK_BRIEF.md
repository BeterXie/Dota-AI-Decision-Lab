## Reader and truth

- Reader action: scan live/upcoming series, enter a series or map, understand result and state, inspect odds/R.O.S.H./draft evidence, and read the AI decision without losing provenance.
- Viewing conditions: desktop data review and narrow mobile browsing, including long Chinese team/event names.
- Required truth: unknown remains unknown; UI must not fabricate odds, R.O.S.H., hero, team, subscription, or AI state. Existing access boundaries and navigation remain intact.
- Refusals: no wholesale old-version restoration, no decorative microcopy, no generic neon AI spectacle, no tiny type used to preserve density, and no unlicensed font dependency.

## Asset and type decisions

- Content-essential assets: official team and hero images identify real competition entities; named hero images establish the three approved brand entrances.
- Reference family: official Dota esports/event products and the owner's earlier match-detail implementation.
- Type voice: IBM Plex Sans / IBM Plex Mono with Noto Sans SC fallback. IBM Plex and Noto are OFL-1.1 fonts suitable for commercial use.
- Rejected type voice: proprietary Dota display fonts and narrow decorative faces with incomplete Chinese coverage.

## Form questions

- Surface roles: home orientation; event discovery; event detail; match evidence and AI decision; performance comparison; review; account, billing, and notification operations; team identity/history.
- Capacity check: validate real Chinese copy, long team names, dense decision reasons, empty/error states, and 360-390 px mobile widths.
- Visual mother object: a live competition signal board, grounded by Aegis/event imagery and real team, hero, score, market, and model evidence.
- Title-removal test: team identities, match state, evidence ordering, and competition imagery must still make each surface recognizably Dota and task-specific.
- Single-canvas counterfactual: fails. This is an interactive multi-route state system, not one static presentation.

## Autonomous fallback

- Least-assumptive choice: tune shared tokens and proven components first; make page-specific changes only where browser evidence shows a hierarchy or interaction defect.
- Unresolved risk: authenticated account/billing states may not be populated locally; verify their public/empty states and preserve existing contracts.
